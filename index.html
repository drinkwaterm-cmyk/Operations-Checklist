Cross-department priorities
Budget & fund codes
Verify expenditures align with correct TEA fund/function/object codes. Miscodings trigger audit findings and TEA sanctions.
Staffing & vacancies
Monitor vacancy rates across all departments. Unfilled positions create service gaps, OT liability, and safety risks.
Safety culture
Track near-miss reports, incident trends, and training completion. A reactive safety record signals systemic gaps to TEA and insurers.
Certifications & renewals
Bus driver CDLs, nutrition manager certifications, boiler licenses — lapses expose the district to liability and program suspension.
Compliance calendar
Maintain a single shared deadline calendar for all state, federal, and regulatory submissions across departments.
Community perception
Operations are customer-facing. Monitor parent/staff complaints about buses, cafeteria, and facilities — these reach the board fast.

The Executive Prompt — end every director meeting with these three asks
To transition from information-gathering to active strategic management, ask each director to deliver three things within two weeks of your first meeting.
Ask 01
The Dashboard
"What are the top 3–5 metrics you look at every Monday to know if your department is winning or losing? I want us to review those same metrics together monthly."
Ask 02
The Org Chart & Pain Points
"Show me your leadership structure. Where are you weak? Who is your rockstar, and who is your bottleneck?"
Ask 03
The Budget Wishlist
"If the Superintendent gave us an extra $250,000 for your department tomorrow, what would you spend it on for the highest return on investment or risk reduction?"
First meeting strategy

You don't need to know how to fix a bus transmission or program a commercial chiller. Your job is governance, risk management, resource allocation, and strategic support. These questions establish your executive lens while respecting each director's technical expertise.
