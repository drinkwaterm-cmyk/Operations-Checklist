Cross-department priorities
Budget & fund codes
Verify expenditures align with correct TEA fund/function/object codes. Miscodings trigger audit findings and TEA sanctions.
Staffing & vacancies
Monitor vacancy rates across all departments. Unfilled positions create service gaps, OT liability, and safety risks.
Safety culture
Track near-miss reports, incident trends, and training completion. A reactive safety record signals systemic gaps to TEA and insurers.
Certifications & renewals
Bus driver CDLs, nutrition manager certifications, boiler licenses — lapses expose the district to liability and program suspension.
Compliance calendar
Maintain a single shared deadline calendar for all state, federal, and regulatory submissions across departments.
Community perception
Operations are customer-facing. Monitor parent/staff complaints about buses, cafeteria, and facilities — these reach the board fast.
